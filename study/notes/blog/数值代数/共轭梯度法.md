# 5共轭梯度法

## 5.1 最速下降法

th5.1.1 这个就是如果A对称正定求解Ax=b就等价于算一个二次泛函的极小值点

**算法 5.1.1**（解对称正定方程组：最速下降法）

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x_0 = \text{初值} \\
& r_0 = b - A x_0; \quad k = 0 \\
& \textbf{while } r_k \neq 0 \\
& \qquad k = k + 1 \\
& \qquad \alpha_{k-1} = \frac{r_{k-1}^{\mathrm{T}} r_{k-1}}{r_{k-1}^{\mathrm{T}} A r_{k-1}} \\
& \qquad x_k = x_{k-1} + \alpha_{k-1} r_{k-1} \\
& \qquad r_k = b - A x_k \\
& \textbf{end}
\end{aligned}
$$
**定理 5.1.2**　设 $A$ 的特征值为 $0 < \lambda_1 \leqslant \cdots \leqslant \lambda_n$，则由上述算法产生的序列 $\{x_k\}$ 满足

$$
\|x_k - x_*\|_A \leqslant \left( \frac{\lambda_n - \lambda_1}{\lambda_n + \lambda_1} \right)^{k} \|x_0 - x_*\|_A ,
$$

其中 $x_* = A^{-1} b$，$\|x\|_A = \sqrt{x^{\mathrm{T}} A x}$。



为了给出这一定理的证明，我们先证一个引理.

**引理 5.1.1** 设 $ A $ 的特征值为 $ 0 < \lambda_1 \le \cdots \le \lambda_n $，$ P(t) $ 是一个 $ t $ 的多项式，则

\[
\| P(A)x \|_A \le \max_{1 \le i \le n} |P(\lambda_i)| \, \| x \|_A, \quad x \in \mathbf{R}^n.
\]

**证明** 设 $ y_1, y_2, \cdots, y_n $ 是 $ A $ 的对应于 $ \lambda_1, \lambda_2, \cdots, \lambda_n $ 的特征向量所构成的 $ \mathbf{R}^n $ 的一组标准正交基，则对任意的 $ x \in \mathbf{R}^n $，有 $ x = \sum\limits_{i=1}^{n} \beta_i y_i $，从而有

\[
x^{\mathrm{T}} P(A) AP(A) x = \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \beta_i P(\lambda_i) y_i \right)^{\mathrm{T}} A \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \beta_i P(\lambda_i) y_i \right)
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
&= \sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_i \beta_i^2 P^2(\lambda_i) \le \max_{1 \le i \le n} P^2(\lambda_i) \sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_i \beta_i^2 \\
&= \max_{1 \le i \le n} P^2(\lambda_i) \, x^{\mathrm{T}} Ax.
\end{aligned}
\]

于是

\[
\| P(A)x \|_A \le \max_{1 \le i \le n} |P(\lambda_i)| \, \| x \|_A,
\]

即引理得证。 $\square$

**定理 5.1.2 的证明** 由 $ x_k $ 满足

\[
\varphi(x_k) \le \varphi(x_{k-1} + \alpha r_{k-1}), \quad \alpha \in \mathbf{R},
\]

并注意到

\[
\varphi(x) + x_*^{\mathrm{T}} A x_* = (x - x_*)^{\mathrm{T}} A (x - x_*), \tag{5.1.4}
\]

就有

\[
\begin{aligned}
& (x_k - x_*)^{\mathrm{T}} A (x_k - x_*) \\
&\le (x_{k-1} + \alpha r_{k-1} - x_*)^{\mathrm{T}} A (x_{k-1} + \alpha r_{k-1} - x_*) \\
&= \left[ (I - \alpha A)(x_{k-1} - x_*) \right]^{\mathrm{T}} A \left[ (I - \alpha A)(x_{k-1} - x_*) \right] \tag{5.1.5}
\end{aligned}
\]

对任意的 $ \alpha \in \mathbf{R} $ 成立。记 $ P_\alpha(t) = 1 - \alpha t $，应用引理 5.1.1，由 (5.1.5) 式可得\[
\begin{aligned}
\| x_k - x_* \|_A &\le \| P_\alpha(A)(x_{k-1} - x_*) \|_A \\
&\le \max_{1 \le i \le n} |P_\alpha(\lambda_i)| \, \| x_{k-1} - x_* \|_A \tag{5.1.6}
\end{aligned}
\]

对一切 $ \alpha \in \mathbf{R} $ 成立，再利用 Chebyshev 多项式的性质，可得

\[
\min_{\alpha} \max_{\lambda_1 \le t \le \lambda_n} |1 - \alpha t| = \frac{\lambda_n - \lambda_1}{\lambda_n + \lambda_1}. \tag{5.1.7}
\]

将 (5.1.7) 式代入 (5.1.6) 式即得

\[
\| x_k - x_* \|_A \le \frac{\lambda_n - \lambda_1}{\lambda_n + \lambda_1} \| x_{k-1} - x_* \|_A,
\]

从而定理得证。 $\square$
**算法 5.2.1**（解对称正定方程组：共轭梯度法）

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x_0 = \text{初值} \\
& r_0 = b - A x_0; \quad k = 0 \\
& \textbf{while } r_k \neq 0 \\
& \qquad k = k + 1 \\
& \qquad \textbf{if } k = 1 \\
& \qquad \qquad p_0 = r_0 \\
& \qquad \textbf{else} \\
& \qquad \qquad \beta_{k-2} = \frac{r_{k-1}^{\mathrm{T}} r_{k-1}}{r_{k-2}^{\mathrm{T}} r_{k-2}} \\
& \qquad \qquad p_{k-1} = r_{k-1} + \beta_{k-2} p_{k-2} \\
& \qquad \textbf{end} \\
& \qquad \alpha_{k-1} = \frac{r_{k-1}^{\mathrm{T}} r_{k-1}}{p_{k-1}^{\mathrm{T}} A p_{k-1}} \\
& \qquad x_k = x_{k-1} + \alpha_{k-1} p_{k-1} \\
& \qquad r_k = r_{k-1} - \alpha_{k-1} A p_{k-1} \\
& \textbf{end} \\
& x = x_k \\
\end{aligned}
$$

##  共轭梯度法(CG)


$
\begin{aligned}
&给定\alpha _{0},第一步d_{0}选择负梯度方向\\
&d_{0}=r_{0}=b-Ax_{0}\\
&\alpha _{0}=\frac{r_{0}^{T}d_{0}}{d_{0}^{T}Ad_{0}}\\
&x_{1}=x_{0}+\alpha _{0}d_{0}\\
&r_{1}=b-Ax_{1}\\
&第二步:\\
&d_{1}不取r_{1},注意到\\
&d_{0}^{T}r_{1}=d_{0}^{T}(r_{0}-\alpha _{0}Ad_{0})\\
&=d_{0}^{T}r_{0}-\alpha _{0}d_{0}^{T}Ad_{0}=0\\
&即d_{0}\perp r_{1}\\
&故可在d_{0},r_{1}张成的平面上找d_{1},s.t. \\
&\phi(x)在平面\Pi _{2}上最小\\
&\Pi _{2}=\{x=x_{1}+\xi r_{1}+\eta d_{0}\}\xi,\eta \in R\\
&考虑f(\xi ,\eta)=\phi(x_{1}+\xi r_{1}+\eta d_{0})\\
&令\frac{\partial f}{\partial \xi }=0\\
&\frac{\partial f}{\partial \eta }=0\\
&\\
&得\\
&\xi r_{1}^{T}Ar_{1}+\eta r_{1}^{T}Ad_{0}=r_{1}^{T}r_{1}\\
&\xi r_{1}^{T}Ad_{0}+\eta d_{0}^{T}Ad_{0}=0\\
&设r_{1}\ne \vec{0},则\xi \ne 0\\
&我门关注的是方向\\
&需求r_{1}+\frac{\eta}{\xi } d_{0}\\
&由Cramer法则可知\\
&=-\frac{d_{0}^{T}Ar_{1}}{d_{0}^{T}Ad_{0}}=:\beta_{0}\\
&\\
&故前进方向可取:\\
&d_{1}=r_{1}+\beta_{0}d_{0}\\
&前进步长与最速下降法类似\\
&\Rightarrow \\
&\alpha _{1}=\frac{r_{1}^{T}d_{1}}{d_{1}^{T}Ad_{1}}\\
&x_{2}=x_{1}+\alpha _{1}d_{1}\\
&r_{2}=b-Ax_{2}\\
&r_{2}\perp d_{1}\\
&以此类推,一般地\\
&\alpha _{k}=\frac{r_{k}^{T}d_{k}}{d_{k}^{T}Ad_{k}}\\
&x_{k+1}=x_{k}+\alpha _{k}d_{k}\\
&r_{k+1}=b-Ax_{k+1}\\
&\beta_{k}=-\frac{d_{k}^{T}Ar_{k+1}}{d_{k}^{T}Ad_{k}}\\
&d_{k+1}=r_{k+1}+\beta_{k}d_{k}\\
&\\\\
&r_{k},\alpha _{k},\beta_{k}有等价的公式\\
&r_{k+1}=r_{k}-\alpha _{k}Ad_{k}\\
&\alpha_{k}=\frac{r_{k}^{T}r_{k}}{d_{k}^{T}Ad_{k}}\\
&\beta=\frac{r_{k+1}^{T}r_{k+1}}{r_{k}^{T}r_{k}}\\
\end{aligned}
$

**定理 5.2.2**　用共轭梯度法计算得到的近似解 $x_k$ 满足

$$
\varphi(x_k) = \min\{\varphi(x):\ x \in x_0 + \mathcal{K}(A, r_0, k)\} \tag{5.2.7}
$$

或

$$
\|x_k - x_*\|_A = \min\{\|x - x_*\|_A:\ x \in x_0 + \mathcal{K}(A, r_0, k)\}, \tag{5.2.8}
$$

## CG的一些性质

$
\begin{aligned}
&th4.3\\
&\forall x\in R^{n},\\
&由m步CG迭代产生的序列\{r_{k}\}_{k=0}^{m}和方向序列\{d_{k}\}_{k=0}^{m}具有如下性质:\\
&\begin{cases}
&(1)r_{k}^{T}d_{i}=i,\forall 0\leq i\leq k-1,1\leq k\leq m\\
&(2)r_{i}^{T}r_{j}=0,\forall 0\leq i\ne j\leq m\\
&(3)d_{i}^{T}Ad_{j}=0,0\leq i,j\leq m\\
&(4)span\{r_{0},r_{1},\ldots,r_{k}\}=\mathcal{K}(A,r_{0},k+1),\forall 0\leq k\leq m\\
\end{cases}\\
&(\mathcal{K}(A,r_{0},k+1)指的是k+1个元素张成的krylov子空间,即\mathcal{K}(A,r_{0},k+1)=span\{r_{0},Ar_{0},\ldots,A^{k}r_{0}\})\\
&证明:\\
&归纳即可得证\\
\end{aligned}
