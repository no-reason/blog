\]

Suppose that a random vector \(\boldsymbol{x}=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)'\in\mathbb R^n\) (\(n\geq2\)) is distributed as a multivariate normal distribution \(N(0,\Sigma)\) with the following joint probability density function
\[
f(\boldsymbol{x})
=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{n/2}\det(\Sigma)^{1/2}}

\quad \boldsymbol{x}\in\mathbb R^n,
\]

where \(\Sigma\) is an \(n\times n\) positive definite matrix.

Let the \((i,j)\) element of \(\Omega=\Sigma^{-1}\) be \(\omega_{ij}\) (\(1\leq i,j\leq n\)).

For \(1\leq i\ne j\leq n\), show that if \(\omega_{ij}=0\), then \(x_i\) and \(x_j\) are conditionally independent when the other elements of \(\boldsymbol{x}\) are given.
2018 — Problem 5
Let \(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{y}\) be two independent random vectors in \(\mathbb R^n\) (\(n\geq3\)). Assume that \(P(\boldsymbol y=0)=0\) and \(\boldsymbol{x}\) has a standard multivariate normal distribution, i.e., \(\boldsymbol{x}\sim N(0,I_n)\).

(a) For any nonzero constant vector \(\boldsymbol{a}\in\mathbb R^n\) satisfying
\[
\|\boldsymbol a\|=(\boldsymbol a'\boldsymbol a)^{1/2}=1,
\]

prove that
\[
\sqrt{n-1}\frac{\boldsymbol a'\boldsymbol x}
{\sqrt{\|\boldsymbol x\|^2-(\boldsymbol a'\boldsymbol x)^2}}
\sim t_{n-1},
\]

where \(t_{n-1}\) stands for a \(t\) distribution with \(n-1\) degrees of freedom.

(b) The sample correlation coefficient between \(\boldsymbol{x}=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)'\) and \(\boldsymbol{y}=(y_1,\ldots,y_n)'\) is defined as
\[
r=
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y)}
{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar x)^2}
\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\bar y)^2}},
\]

where
\[
\bar x=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i}{n},\qquad
\bar y=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}y_i}{n}.
\]

Show that
\[
\sqrt{n-2}\frac{r}{\sqrt{1-r^2}}\sim t_{n-2}.
\]

2020 — Problem 4

1. \(X\) and \(Y\) are independent;
2. both \(X\) and \(Y\) have mean zero and finite (nonvanishing) second moments;
3. the distribution of \(Z\) is invariant under the rotation counter-clockwise around the origin by an angle \(\theta\) not a multiple of \(90\) degrees.
Show that \(X\) and \(Y\) must be normal random variables with the same variance.


笔者起初以为直接写成积分进行强行计算即可solve这些问题。但是经过实际操作，笔者才发现这样操作的计算难度十分大。

经过学习笔者了解到，这类问题可以利用正态分布的正交分解来进行简单计算。故笔者将笔者的学习笔记整理如下，供大家参考。


## 方法抽象和原理

$
\begin{aligned}
&上面的问题可以抽象为如下几个命题:\\
&1.如果我们已知两个联合正态变量X,Y\\
&如何计算E[X|Y]\\
&\\
&常规的计算需要我们去计算联合分布的积分\\
&\\
&但是一种自然的想法是:\\
&如果我们可以把X分解为两个部分\\;
&i.e. X=aY+(X-aY)\\
&其中X-aY和Y独立\\
&这样\\
&E[X|Y]=E[aY+(X-aY)|Y]=E[aY|Y]+E[X-aY|Y]=aY+E[X-aY]\\
&\\
&所以问题就变成了如何找到一个a使得X-aY和Y独立\\
&由于X,Y联合正态\\
&\\
&因此我们只需找a,s.t. \\
&cov(X-aY,Y)=0\\
&\iff E(X-aY)Y-E(X-aY)E(Y)=0\\
&\iff E(XY)-aE(Y^2)-E(X)E(Y)+aE(Y)^2=0\\
&\iff Cov(X,Y)-aVar(Y)=0\\
&\iff a=Cov(X,Y)/Var(Y)\\
&\\
&故知取a=Cov(X,Y)/Var(Y)即可完成正交分解\\
&此时E[X|Y]=aY+E[X-aY]=Cov(X,Y)/Var(Y)Y+E[X]-Cov(X,Y)/Var(Y)E[Y]\\
&=EX+\frac{Cov(X,Y)}{Var(Y)}(Y-E[Y])\\
&\\
&2.标准Guass向量在任何单位方向上的投影都与它在坐标轴上的投影同分布\\
&\\
&
\end{aligned}
$

## 应用


$
\begin{aligned}
&下面我们利用上面的方法来解决最初的一系列问题:\\
\end{aligned}
$

2010 — Problem 1
Let \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_n\) be i.i.d. random variables with \(Z_i\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)\). Find
\[
E\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_i\mid Z_1-Z_2+Z_3\right).
\]

$
\begin{aligned}
&设T=Z_{1}-Z_{2}+Z_{3}\\
&下面考虑\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}=aT+(\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}-aT)\\
&则我们只需寻找a,s.t.\\
&Cov(\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}-aT,T)=0\\
&由上述结论可知\\
&a=\frac{Cov(\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i},T)}{Var(T,T)}\\
&Cov(\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i},T)=Cov(Z_{1}-Z_{2}+Z_{3},Z_{1}+Z_{2}+Z_{3})\\
&=Var(Z_{1}+Z_{3})-Var(Z_{2})=2\sigma^2-\sigma^2=\sigma^2\\
&Var(T)=Var(Z_{1}-Z_{2}+Z_{3})=3\sigma^2\\
&\Rightarrow a=\frac{1}{3}\\
&\\
&故知道E[\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}\mid T]=E[aT+(\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}-aT)\mid T]=aT+E[\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}-aT]\\
&=\frac{1}{3}T+n\mu-\frac{1}{3}\mu\\
&=\frac{(3n-1)\mu+T}{3}\\
\end{aligned}
$
